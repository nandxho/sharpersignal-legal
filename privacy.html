Privacy Policy

SharpSignal Publisher · Last updated: 8 November 2026

1. Who we are

SharpSignal Publisher is a personal content-publishing tool operated by Victor Hugo Luis Jiménez. Contact: toribiu@gmail.com.

2. What data the application handles

The Application connects to TikTok only after the account owner completes TikTok's OAuth authorisation. It then handles:

DataPurpose
TikTok OAuth access and refresh tokensTo publish videos to the authorised account
Open ID and display nameTo confirm which account is authorised
Video files created by the operatorTo upload them to TikTok

The Application does not collect followers, direct messages, viewing history, contact lists, or any data about other TikTok users.

3. How data is stored

Tokens and credentials are stored locally on the operator's own computer, in files readable only by that user account. There is no server-side database and no cloud storage of TikTok user data. Tokens are refreshed automatically and can be revoked at any time from TikTok's Settings → Security and permissions.

4. Data sharing

No personal data is sold, rented, or shared with third parties. The only outbound transfer is the video file and its caption, sent to TikTok's official API in order to publish it, plus public domain photographs used as backgrounds.

5. Retention

Tokens are kept only while the integration is in use. Deleting the local credential file, or revoking access in TikTok, ends the connection immediately. Videos remain in the operator's local archive.

6. Your rights

Because the Application only accesses the authorising account, the account owner can revoke consent at any time from TikTok's settings. For any request about your data, write to toribiu@gmail.com.

7. Children

The Application is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly process their data.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated; the date at the top reflects the current version.
