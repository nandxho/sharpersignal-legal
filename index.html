What this application does

SharpSignal Publisher is a personal content-publishing tool. It lets its operator upload short-form vertical videos to a TikTok account that the operator has explicitly authorised through TikTok's official OAuth flow.

It does not browse TikTok, read other users' content, send messages, or act on behalf of anyone other than the account owner who granted permission.
